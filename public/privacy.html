How Saathi handles student data

Saathi is an automated learning tool, not a human friend, school counsellor, doctor or emergency service.

What is saved

Your login account, chosen board, selected subjects and daily study target; study tasks and due dates; private note titles and note bodies; your weekly schedule; personal memories the learner explicitly saves; and aggregate counts of practice questions attempted and answered correctly.

What is not saved in Saathi's database

Your tutoring chat transcripts and the answers you selected in practice are not stored in the application database. Messages sent to the AI tutor are transmitted to Cloudflare Workers AI for processing, where Cloudflare's separate data-handling terms apply. Do not enter passwords, addresses, phone numbers or other sensitive details in AI questions.

Uploaded student material and media

The My uploads feature reads TXT, Markdown, CSV, JSON, VTT and SRT files in your own browser and lets you review the extracted text before asking a question. Supported images (PNG, JPEG, WebP) and one learner-selected paused frame from an MP4 or WebM video can be transmitted only after you explicitly approve analysis. The frame is not the entire video and contains no automatically extracted audio. Vision analysis uses the Cloudflare Workers AI image model, if available and permitted by provider terms and available account quota. Image recognition may misread handwritten notes or diagrams. Source files, frames, and model replies are not automatically written to Saathi D1. Uploaded study material can still be processed by the AI provider when you choose Analyze. Avoid photographs showing addresses, account details, identifiable people or sensitive personal information. At present, full PDF, Word and PowerPoint extraction is not commissioned; do not assume these files were read. You can instead paste permitted text into Study links.

Study links and YouTube transcripts

When you explicitly submit a supported educational webpage, Saathi temporarily retrieves a limited excerpt of public HTML or plain text. Other pages may block access or be unsupported. A YouTube link is not proof that Saathi watched the video: the video’s spoken text is not automatically downloaded. If you paste video captions or a study passage, only the excerpts needed for your question are sent with that question to Cloudflare Workers AI. The original text, source URL and AI explanation are not stored in Saathi's database unless you explicitly choose Save as note or Create a revision task. Notes and tasks then follow the normal learner-only account rules. YouTube captions can be incomplete or inaccurate, and visual demonstrations are not understood from transcript text alone. Do not paste sensitive personal data or material you are not permitted to use.

Your AI memory choices

You are in control of personal memories. Ordinary chat does not create memories automatically. You can explicitly save a short goal, learning preference or interest under Mission Control → AI memory. Each memory has a separate opt-in switch: only memories you enable for future AI conversations may be transmitted to Cloudflare Workers AI with your questions. Other saved memories stay in your account and are not sent to the AI. You can read, edit, disable AI recall, export or delete every memory. Do not store passwords, contact details, sensitive medical information or disclosures about harm as memories. Provider-managed backups may retain deleted data under separate retention policies.

Parent and administrator views

The parent account can see aggregate task and practice progress for the linked learner. The administrator can see operational counts. Neither role has an application feature for reading private tutoring chats, private personal memories, personal notes, detailed task titles or practice answer choices.

About the school syllabus

Foundation lessons are introductory examples only. They are not a certified or complete Class 12 curriculum and should be checked against the learner's current prescribed textbooks and teacher guidance.

Creative artwork and voice

Your drawings stay on the device in the current browser session until you download them. Saathi does not upload or persist the canvas. Microphone listening begins only when you tap Speak and accept browser permission. Depending on your browser, speech recognition may send audio to a browser provider. If you prefer, type instead. Spoken replies use browser speech synthesis where supported.

Reminders

The in-app reminders check your schedule only while Mission Control is open. Optional phone-calendar exports are initiated by you; after importing, your phone's calendar app—not Saathi—controls recurring background alerts. No automatic SMS, email or push reminders are sent.

Support and safety

If you are unsafe or in immediate danger, contact a safe trusted adult or your local emergency services. In India, the emergency number is 112. If a parent or guardian is unsafe, speak with another trusted adult or appropriate support service. Saathi cannot contact a person or send an alert for you.

Data deletion and account help

In Mission Control → My data, a signed-in student can export or erase study notes, scheduled activities, practice counters, academic preferences, opted-in memories and tasks without deleting their login. The export must be kept private. The current app does not delete the account itself or guarantee immediate removal from provider-managed backups; account removal still requires an administrator. This is a limited staging trial, not a complete recovery service.
