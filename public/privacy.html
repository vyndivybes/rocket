How Saathi handles student data

Saathi is an automated learning tool, not a human friend, school counsellor, doctor or emergency service.

What is saved

Your account, chosen board, selected subjects and daily study target; study tasks and due dates; and only the counts of practice questions attempted and answered correctly.

What is not saved in Saathi's database

Your tutoring chat transcripts and the answers you selected in practice are not stored in the application database. Messages sent to the AI tutor are transmitted to Cloudflare Workers AI for processing, where Cloudflare's separate data-handling terms apply. Do not enter passwords, addresses, phone numbers or other sensitive details in AI questions.

Parent and administrator views

The parent account can see aggregate task and practice progress for the linked learner. The administrator can see operational counts. Neither role has an application feature for reading private tutoring chats, detailed task titles or practice answer choices.

About the school syllabus

Foundation lessons are introductory examples only. They are not a certified or complete Class 12 curriculum and should be checked against the learner's current prescribed textbooks and teacher guidance.

Support and safety

If you are unsafe or in immediate danger, contact a safe trusted adult or your local emergency services. In India, the emergency number is 112. If a parent or guardian is unsafe, speak with another trusted adult or appropriate support service. Saathi cannot contact a person or send an alert for you.

Data deletion and account help

For the current family test, ask the account administrator to remove the saved study tasks, practice counters, academic preferences or entire account from the dedicated database. Self-service deletion and recovery are not yet available, so this is a limited staging trial. Avoid entering information you would not want stored as a study task.
